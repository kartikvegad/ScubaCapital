1. Executive Summary — What the Developer Must Know
SCUBA CAPITAL PVT LTD is being treated in this document as an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (MFD). The key objective is to build the website and social-media ecosystem so that marketing activity does not accidentally cross the line from permitted distribution/education into misleading advertising or unregistered investment-advisory activity.
•	Use the registered entity name and clearly identify the business as an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor.
•	Do not use 'Investment Adviser', 'Financial Adviser/Advisor', 'Wealth Adviser', 'Wealth Manager', 'Financial Planner', 'Consultant' or similar titles unless the relevant SEBI registration permits it.
•	Do not promise, assure or imply guaranteed/risk-free returns from mutual funds.
•	Do not publish scheme-specific return projections, indicative yield, indicative portfolio or assured-return language.
•	Do not create scheme-specific promotional creatives using an AMC logo/name without prior written AMC approval.
•	For securities-market-related social-media content, comply with SEBI's 26 February 2026 EoDI circular effective from 1 May 2026: registered name + applicable registration/identification details must be prominently disclosed as prescribed.
•	Build an internal approval workflow: every post, reel, video, blog, landing page and paid advertisement should have an owner, source, approval status and archive copy.
•	Maintain records of published content, approvals, source documents, campaign dates and changes.

4. Prohibited / High-Risk Naming and Positioning
AMFI's 2026 MFD Master Circular states that an MFD should not use titles that create an impression of performing a role for which it is not registered.

IMPORTANT: Do not assume that changing a word in a website headline makes an advisory activity permissible. The substance of the service and how it is presented matter.
5. Website & Advertising Content Rules
The SEBI advertisement code requires communications to be accurate, true, fair, clear, complete, unambiguous and concise, and prohibits misleading, biased, deceptive or exaggerated statements.
Content type	Developer rule
Returns	Never say 'guaranteed', 'assured', 'risk-free', 'fixed return' or equivalent for mutual funds.
Forecasts	Do not publish future NAV/return forecasts or scheme-specific return promises.
Risk	Do not hide risk in fine print while making a strong promotional claim.
Comparisons	Use only fair, comparable products/schemes and complete facts; avoid disparaging competitors.
Testimonials	Do not use prohibited testimonials/rankings in mutual-fund advertisements; obtain compliance approval before using any client quote.
Celebrities/influencers	Do not use celebrities in mutual-fund advertisements. Do not create an influencer campaign that bypasses regulated communication rules.
Past performance	Treat performance content as high-risk and subject it to applicable SEBI/AMFI performance-advertising requirements and approval.
Scheme facts	Use current SID/SAI/KIM/factsheet/AMC-approved material as the source of truth.

6. Standard Mutual Fund Risk Warning
MANDATORY: For scheme advertisements, use the prescribed standard warning exactly as applicable: 'Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.' Do not rewrite it casually.
For audio-visual mutual-fund advertising, the standard warning must be clearly visible and accompanied by clear voice-over reiteration for at least 5 seconds, subject to the applicable current requirements.
Important distinction: a general MFD corporate/service communication is not automatically identical to an AMC's scheme advertisement. The compliance treatment depends on the content. When in doubt, treat a scheme-specific promotion as an advertisement and route it for approval.

8. Social Media — 2026 EoDI Rules
SEBI Circular HO/(79)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC dated 26 February 2026 applies to social-media platforms including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Telegram, Reddit and similar platforms. It became effective from 1 May 2026.
•	The social-media home/profile page must prominently disclose the registered name and applicable registration number/identification as prescribed.
•	For securities-market-related videos/content, the required registered name and registration number/identification must appear at the beginning of the content as prescribed.
•	If an entity has multiple SEBI registrations, the profile must provide a link to the website page listing all registrations; the content must identify the capacity/registration relevant to that content.
•	Content must not be false, misleading, biased or deceptive.
•	Content must not exploit investor inexperience or knowledge gaps.
•	Content must not promise or guarantee assured or risk-free returns, directly or indirectly.
•	Do not use SEBI's logo or make references suggesting endorsement by SEBI.
•	Do not link/associate with persons making prohibited investment-return claims or unauthorised advice in a way that creates a regulatory concern.
IMPORTANT: The EoDI disclosure is an additional social-media identity layer. It does not replace the AMFI MFD identity/tagline or other applicable advertisement requirements.

12. AMC Logos, Scheme Names & Marketing Material
•	For scheme-specific marketing, use material supplied by the AMC wherever possible.
•	Do not independently redesign an AMC's scheme creative or use its name/logo/mark without prior written approval where AMFI/SEBI rules require such approval.
•	Keep the approval email/letter and the exact final creative in the compliance archive.
•	Do not use outdated factsheets, scheme names, expense ratios, risk-o-meters, portfolio data or performance numbers.
•	If a scheme changes category/name/benchmark or other material feature, update/remove old website and social content promptly.
13. Lead Forms, WhatsApp & Data Handling
•	Collect only information necessary for the stated purpose.
•	Display a privacy notice before or at the point of collection.
•	Record consent where required and do not silently add users to unrelated marketing lists.
•	Do not publish screenshots containing PAN, folio number, bank details, mobile number or other client information.
•	Do not share investor data with group companies for cross-marketing unless permitted and properly disclosed/consented under applicable requirements.
•	Use secure transmission/storage, access controls and retention/deletion procedures.
•	Marketing CRM exports should have role-based access and audit trails.
14. SEO, AI-Generated Content & Website Automation
•	AI-generated content is not exempt from SEBI/AMFI rules. Human review is mandatory before publication.
•	Do not allow AI tools to invent returns, rankings, fund facts, tax outcomes or regulatory claims.
•	Every statistic should have a source and 'as on' date where appropriate.
•	Do not use AI-generated testimonials or synthetic client experiences as if they were real.
•	Do not create SEO pages targeting 'best mutual fund', 'guaranteed return', 'sure-shot SIP', 'risk-free investment' or similar misleading search terms.
•	Automated website widgets must have a compliance owner and an emergency disable function.

19. Final Management Instruction
The website developer should not independently decide whether a financial claim is compliant. The developer's role is to implement approved content and technical controls. SCUBA CAPITAL management/compliance should own the final decision on regulatory wording, scheme-specific marketing, performance claims, paid campaigns, social-media content and disclosures. The safest operating model is 'approve once, publish many' using controlled templates.
